# Project governance

PocketCtrl was created by Kylan O'Connor at [Iterative](https://tryiterative.com), which owns, manages, and maintains the official project.

Iterative stewards the official repositories, releases, roadmap, project policies, and PocketCtrl branding. Kylan is PocketCtrl's creator and lead maintainer. Project maintainers have final responsibility for accepting contributions and deciding the direction of official releases.

Community discussion, issues, and pull requests are welcome. This governance model may evolve as the contributor community grows; material changes will be documented in this file.

Licensing decisions are governed by [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md), and use of PocketCtrl's name and logos is governed by [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).
